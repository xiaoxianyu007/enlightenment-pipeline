Episode 1: The Western Hegemon — Duke Mu of Qin

In the seventh century before Christ, on the western frontier of the Zhou world, a young duke named Ying Renhao came to power in the state of Qin. His people herded horses on the windswept Loess Plateau, hardened by centuries of skirmishing with the Rong barbarians. The central states dismissed them as semi-savages, barely more civilized than the nomads they fought. But Duke Mu had a vision that stretched far beyond the dusty garrison towns of the western marches. He knew that if Qin was to survive in a world of expanding kingdoms, it must learn from the east even as it conquered the west.

His first and boldest act was to marry a princess of Jin, the dominant state of the central plain, forging a dynastic alliance that brought Qin into the great power game for the first time. Through this marriage, Duke Mu gained not just a bride but access to the political and military knowledge of the Jin court. When a famine struck Jin in 647 BC, Mu dispatched grain ships down the Wei River and up the Yellow River, feeding hundreds of thousands of Jin commoners in an act of calculated generosity that stunned the aristocratic courts of the eastern states. Two years later, when Qin itself faced famine, Jin repaid the kindness with an army at the border. Duke Mu was captured in battle at Hanyuan, his trust shattered, his army in ruins.

But the duke escaped captivity through a twist of diplomatic fortune, and he learned the hardest lesson of statecraft: trust no one. Over the following decades, Duke Mu turned his gaze westward. He seduced the Rong king with gifts of women, musicians, and luxuries, softening him until the proud chieftain had forgotten the arts of war. When the Rong were ripe, Qin struck — conquering a dozen Rong tribes and absorbing their lands, their horses, their warriors. The central states called Qin barbaric, but it was precisely this ability to absorb and transform barbarian strength into Qin strength that would define the state for centuries to come.

When Duke Mu died in 621 BC, he was buried with 177 of his finest subjects — a human sacrifice recorded with horror by the historians of later ages. The practice spoke to the archaic, uncompromising culture of Qin. But he left behind a state that had expanded from a dust-blown garrison to a territorial power that the eastern kingdoms could no longer ignore. The foundation had been laid — a Qin that was strong enough to survive, ambitious enough to learn, and ruthless enough to do what others would not.

第一集：西陲霸主——秦穆公

公元前七世纪，在周朝世界的西部边陲，一位名叫嬴任好的年轻公爵继承了秦国的统治权。他的族人在黄土高原上牧马，经过数百年与戎狄的战争磨砺出了一身硬骨。中原诸侯视他们为半开化的野蛮人，文明程度并不比他们作战的游牧部落高多少。但秦穆公的雄心远非西部边陲的军镇所能容纳。他深知，在一个列国扩张的时代，秦若要存续，就必须在征服西戎的同时，向东方学习。

他最大胆的第一步，是迎娶了中原霸主晋国的公主，建立了一段宗室联姻，使秦国首次进入了列强博弈的舞台。通过这桩婚姻，秦穆公得到的不仅是一位妻子，更是通往晋国宫廷政治与军事知识的渠道。公元前647年，晋国遭遇大饥荒，秦穆公派出粮船顺渭河东下、再溯黄河而上，赈济了数十万晋国百姓。这一精心计算的慷慨之举，让东方列国的贵族宫廷为之震动。然而两年后，当秦国自己也遭遇饥荒时，晋国以边境大军回报了当年的恩情。秦穆公在韩原之战中被俘，信任被撕碎，大军溃败。

但秦穆公凭借外交命运的转折逃脱了囚禁，也学会了治国最残酷的一课：永远不要信任任何人。此后数十年，秦穆公将目光转向西方。他向西戎之王赠送美女、乐师和珍玩，日复一日地消磨其意志，直到这位骄傲的酋长彻底遗忘了战争的艺术。时机成熟后，秦军出击——连克十余个西戎部落，吞并了他们的土地、战马和勇士。中原诸侯嘲笑秦人野蛮，但恰恰是秦人吸收异族力量并将其转化为自身实力的能力，在几个世纪之后定义了秦国的命运。

秦穆公于公元前621年去世，177位最能干的臣民随他殉葬——这桩人殉之事被后世史官以惊惧之笔记下。这仪式折射出秦文化中古旧而毫无余地的一面。但他留下了一个从风沙之地扩张为国土强权的秦国，东方列国再也无法忽视。基础已经奠定——这个秦国足够强大以生存，足够好学以求变，也足够冷酷以行他人之不敢行。

Episode 2: The Road to Reform — From Silence to Rebirth

For over two hundred years after Duke Mu's death, Qin vanished from the great stage of Chinese history. The historical records go almost silent — a century of internal power struggles, noble infighting, and stagnation while the eastern states raced ahead. Wei adopted the military reforms of Wu Qi, Chu expanded southward along the Yangtze, and the other kingdoms grew richer and stronger while Qin withered on the vine. Successive Qin dukes came and went, some assassinated, others exiled, none leaving a mark worthy of recording.

The turning point came in 384 BC when Duke Xian ascended the throne after decades in exile at the Wei court. He had seen how Wei had reformed itself — new infantry tactics, bureaucratic administration, canals, granaries. Upon his return to Qin, he abolished the barbaric practice of human sacrifice that had accompanied ducal burials since the time of Duke Mu. He established marketplaces, standardized weights and measures, and most crucially, implemented a household registration system that gave the state direct knowledge of its population for the first time. But Duke Xian was old and his reforms were modest. The real transformation required a younger man, a hungrier man, and an idea that would overturn everything the old aristocracy held sacred.

On Duke Xian's deathbed in 362 BC, his twenty-one-year-old son Ying Quliang knelt beside him and listened to his final whisper: "The eastern lords treat Qin as a barbarian state. This is the greatest shame our people have ever known. Restore our honor." When the young man rose as Duke Xiao, he carried those words like a brand on his heart. Two months into his reign, he issued a proclamation that would change the course of Chinese civilization: any man — regardless of birth, regardless of origin — who could devise a plan to strengthen Qin would be granted high office, lands, and titles. The Qiu Xian Ling, the Edict of Seeking Talent, was nailed to every garrison gate from the Wei River to the Long Mountains.

第二集：变法之路——从沉寂到复兴

秦穆公去世后的两百余年间，秦国从中国历史的大舞台上销声匿迹。史书记载几乎陷入死寂——长达一个世纪的内部权力斗争、贵族倾轧和停滞不前，而东方列国却早已跑在了前面。魏国推行了吴起的军事改革，楚国沿长江向南扩张，其他诸侯国日益富强，而秦国则在藤蔓上渐渐枯萎。历代秦公来了又走，有的被刺杀，有的被流放，没有一位留下值得史书记载的痕迹。

转折点出现在公元前384年，在魏国宫廷流亡了数十年的秦献公回国即位。他在魏国亲眼目睹了改革的力量——新的步兵战术、官僚行政系统、运河与粮仓。回到秦国后，他立即废除了自秦穆公以来一直伴随国君葬礼的野蛮殉葬制度。他建立了市场，统一了度量衡，最关键的是推行了户籍登记制度，使国家首次能直接掌握其人口规模。但献公年事已高，改革规模有限。真正的变革需要更年轻、更饥渴的人，以及一个足以颠覆旧贵族一切信条的思想。

公元前362年，献公弥留之际，他二十一岁的儿子嬴渠梁跪在榻前，听到了父亲最后的低语："东方诸侯视秦为戎狄之国，此乃吾族之最大耻辱。收复尊严！"当这个年轻人站起身、成为秦孝公时，他将父亲的话深深烙印在心上。即位两个月后，他颁布了一道将改变中华文明进程的诏书：无论出身、无论来历，任何人若能提出使秦国强盛之策，都将被授予高官、封地、爵位。求贤令被钉在从渭水到陇山的每一个军镇大门上。

Episode 3: Duke Xiao and Shang Yang — The Meeting That Changed China

The Edict of Seeking Talent flew across the warring world like a spark in dry grass. In the state of Wei, a minor official named Gongsun Yang — later to be known as Shang Yang — read the proclamation and recognized his destiny. Here was a man of extraordinary intellect, a student of the Legalist master Li Kui, armed with radical ideas about how to organize human society. He had been serving as a tutor to the prime minister of Wei, but Wei had no use for revolutionaries. Qin, it seemed, was desperate for one.

Shang Yang made the long journey westward through the Han Valley Pass, carrying only a few scrolls of Legalist texts and a burning conviction that the old aristocratic order was a cancer eating the Zhou world from within. Duke Xiao received him at the Qin capital of Yueyang, a modest wooden-pillared hall vastly inferior to the bronze-studded palaces of the eastern lords. The audience was awkward at first. Shang Yang spoke of the sage kings Yao and Shun, of Confucian benevolence and Daoist non-action. Duke Xiao yawned openly and dismissed him. At the second audience, Shang Yang extolled the middle way — moderate reforms, balanced governance. The duke remained unmoved. This young scholar was a bore like all the others.

But Shang Yang had been testing the duke. At the third meeting, he finally revealed his true doctrine. "The state," he declared, "has only two purposes: agriculture and war. The farmer produces grain, the soldier produces victory. Everything else — art, philosophy, ritual, leisure — is decay. Reward the farmer when his harvest exceeds quota, and he will tear open new fields. Reward the soldier for every severed head he brings, and he will march into Hell unafraid. Punish all failures without mercy, without regard to rank. The law is the law, and it falls equally on the duke and the peasant." Duke Xiao leaned forward. He did not sleep that night, nor the next. For three days and nights, the two men debated the architecture of a new kind of state — a state that ran on fear and greed, not loyalty and blood.

第三集：孝公与商鞅——改变中国的会面

求贤令如火星落入枯草，飞遍了战火纷飞的天下。在魏国，一位名叫公孙鞅——后称商鞅——的下级官员读到了这份诏书，从中看到了自己的命运。这是一个拥有超凡智慧的人，法家大师李悝的学生，脑中心怀一套重组人类社会的激进思想。他一直在魏国宰相门下做门客，但魏国并不需要革命者。而秦国，似乎正时刻渴望着一个。

商鞅带着几卷法家典籍和一种燃烧的信念——旧贵族秩序是从内部吞噬周朝世界的癌——踏上了穿越函谷关的漫长西行之路。秦孝公在栎阳会见了他。这座简陋的木柱殿堂，远不及东方诸侯的青铜镶饰宫殿。开头的气氛颇为尴尬。商鞅大谈尧舜圣王、儒家仁政、道家的无为而治，秦孝公当众打起了哈欠，打发了他。第二次觐见，商鞅又宣扬了中庸之道和温和改革，秦孝公仍然无动于衷。这个年轻学者跟其他儒生一样无趣。

但商鞅一直在试探这位年轻的国君。第三次会见，他终于亮出了真正的思想。他宣告："国家只有两个目的：农耕与战争。农民产粮，战士取胜。其余的一切——艺术、哲学、礼乐、闲暇——皆为腐朽。当农民的收成超过定量时就给予重赏，他将为你去开垦新的土地。当战士每带回一颗敌军首级就赏爵一级，他将毫不畏惧地踏入地狱。所有的失败者都要受到无情的惩罚，不论地位高低。法律就是法律，不论公侯还是庶民，一视同仁。"秦孝公向前倾了倾身。那一夜，他没有合眼，第二天也是。连续三天三夜，两人探讨着一种新型国家的构造——以恐惧与贪婪为驱动，而非忠诚与血缘。

Episode 4: The Reforms of Shang Yang — Remaking a Nation

Duke Xiao named Shang Yang the chancellor of Qin and gave him absolute authority to remake the state. What followed between 356 and 350 BC was the most sweeping program of social engineering the Chinese world had ever seen. Shang Yang's reforms struck at everything the old order held sacred.

First came the registration. Every household in Qin was counted, recorded, and organized into groups of five or ten families mutually responsible for each other's conduct — the bao-jia system. If one family harbored a criminal, all ten would be punished. If one family failed to report a neighbor's evasion of taxes, all ten would lose their land. This turned every village into an informant network, every neighbor into a policeman.

Second, the military merit system. The old hereditary nobility had claimed rank by blood for centuries. Shang Yang announced that henceforth, noble rank — of which he created twenty grades — could only be earned through battlefield performance. A foot soldier who presented one enemy head was promoted one grade and exempted from corvée labor. An officer who killed the enemy commander in battle was raised five grades. But retreat was punished with the execution of the entire unit. This system created a terrifying engine: an army whose every soldier had a personal financial and social stake in victory, and a mortal fear of defeat.

Third, agriculture was placed above all other occupations. Farmers who exceeded production quotas were exempted from forced labor. Merchants and artisans were taxed punitively, and their sons were forbidden from taking civil examinations. The message was unambiguous: the state values only grain and blood. In the streets of Yueyang, the old noble families raged. The crown prince himself, Ying Si, violated the law — and Shang Yang ordered his tutors, Prince Qian and Gongsun Jia, to be mutilated as punishment, since the prince himself was immune as heir. The message shattered the old order: not even the heir to the throne was above the law.

第四集：商鞅变法——重塑一个国家

秦孝公任命商鞅为左庶长，给予他绝对权力来重塑这个国家。在此后的公元前356年到350年间发生的事，是一场中国前所未见、最大规模的社会工程改造。商鞅的变法击碎了旧秩序所奉若神明的一切。

首先是登记造册。秦国的每一户家庭都被清点、记录并编入一组——五户或十户互相担保彼此行为的保甲连坐制度。若一家藏匿罪犯，十家连坐。若一家未举报邻居逃税，十家一并失地。这让每一个村庄变成了告密之网，每一个邻居都成了警察。

其次是军功爵制度。数百年来，世袭贵族以血统维持等级。商鞅宣告，从此以后，爵位——他创立了二十个等级——只能凭战场表现获得。一个步兵带一颗敌军首级归来，升一级，免除徭役。一位军官阵前斩杀敌将，连升五级。但临阵退缩者，全队处斩。这个制度创造了一台可怕的战争引擎——一支军队中，每一位士兵的胜利都跟个人财富与社会地位挂钩，失败则意味着死亡的恐惧。

第三，重农抑商。超过生产定额的农夫免服徭役。商人、工匠被征收高额惩罚税，其子弟不准参加选官考试。传递的信息毫不含糊：国家只认粮食与鲜血。栎阳街头，旧贵族怒不可遏。太子嬴驷本人犯了法——商鞅下令将太子的师傅公子虔和公孙贾施以黥刑，因为太子作为继承人不可动刑。这一判决击碎了旧秩序的最后一道防线：即便是王位继承人也逃脱不了法的制裁。

Episode 5: The Legalist State — Qin's Transformation

The reforms did not immediately silence Qin's critics. In the fourth year of the new order, thousands of malcontents flooded the streets of Yueyang, denouncing the new laws as tyranny. Duke Xiao grew nervous. Had he bet his kingdom on a madman? But Shang Yang was unshakable. "A great ruler," he told the duke, "does not govern by popular opinion. The masses cannot see beyond tomorrow. Only the state can plan for a century. Silence the protesters, and in ten years they will thank you." He had every protester arrested, and not a single voice rose in dissent again.

By 350 BC, Qin had moved its capital from Yueyang eastward to Xianyang, closer to the central plain, a symbol of ambition and growing confidence. The new city was laid out on a grid pattern years before such planning appeared anywhere else in China. Granaries rose beside armories, law courts stood beside weapons workshops. The fields of Qin, drained and irrigated by forced peasant labor, began producing surpluses that no other state could match. Foreign laborers were invited to settle uncultivated land, swelling the state's tax base.

In 341 BC, Qin's new army proved itself at last. Wei, the superpower that had humiliated Qin for decades, sent an invading force westward. The Qin army, now driven by the hunger for heads and ranks, met them at the border and delivered a crushing defeat. The Wei commander was captured, and Wei ceded the strategic Hexi corridor — the fertile land west of the Yellow River that Wei had held for nearly a century. Qin had broken the encirclement. As the news reached the eastern courts, laughter turned to silence. The barbarian state had stopped being a joke.

And yet the transformation was not yet complete. In 338 BC, Duke Xiao fell gravely ill. Shang Yang had made enemies of every powerful family in Qin, and he knew that when his protector died, the wolves would circle. On the duke's deathbed, the old partnership faced its final test. Duke Xiao, in a moment of desperate sincerity, offered Shang Yang the throne itself — step into my place and rule Qin. Shang Yang refused. He was a reformer, not a usurper. It was, perhaps, the most fatal decision of his life.

第五集：法治秦国——蜕变

变法之初并没有立刻堵住反对者的嘴。新政第四年，数以千计的不满者涌入栎阳街头，怒斥新法为暴政。秦孝公不安了起来。他难道把整个国家押在了一个疯子身上吗？但商鞅毫不动摇。他对孝公说："伟大的统治者不以民意治国。庶民只知明日之事，唯国家能规划百年之业。让抗议者闭嘴，十年之后他们会感激你。"所有抗议者统统被捕入狱，从此秦国再也没有发出一声异议。

到公元前350年，秦国将都城从栎阳向东迁往咸阳，更靠近中原，这是一个野心与不断增长的信心的象征。新城以网格规划布局，比中国其他地区出现此类城市规划早了不知多少年。粮仓紧挨着兵器库，法庭紧挨军工作坊。秦国的田野经过强制民夫排水灌溉的改造后，开始产出其他任何诸侯国都无法比肩的盈余。外来劳工被邀请迁入荒地定居开垦，国家的税基由此不断膨胀。

公元前341年，秦国的新军终于证明了自己。那个羞辱了秦国数十年的霸主魏国，派出一支入侵大军西进。如今被斩首换爵的欲望驱动的秦军，在边境迎头痛击，大获全胜。魏军主将被俘，魏国被迫割让河西走廊——那片肥沃的黄河以西之地，魏国已占据近百年。秦国打破了封锁。当捷报传到东方列国的宫廷时，嘲笑变为了沉默。这个蛮夷国家不再是个笑话了。

然而，彻底的蜕变尚未完成。公元前338年，秦孝公病重垂危。商鞅已将秦国每一个有势力的家族都变成了敌人，他深知一旦保护者去世，狼群便会围拢过来。孝公在病榻之上，这对老搭档面临最后的考验。孝公出于绝望的真诚，要将王位传给商鞅——"代我之位，替我治理这个国家"。商鞅拒绝了。他是改革者，不是篡位者。这也许是他一生中最致命的选择。

Episode 6: The Death of Shang Yang and the Rise of the King

Duke Xiao died in the summer of 338 BC, and the wolves indeed circled. The crown prince Ying Si — the same man whose tutors had been mutilated by Shang Yang's orders — now ascended to the throne as King Huiwen of Qin. (He was the first Qin ruler to claim the title of king rather than duke, a declaration that Qin now stood equal to any power in the Zhou world.) The old noble families, led by Prince Qian — still bearing the scars of his punishment — demanded vengeance. Shang Yang was accused of plotting rebellion and fled.

He fled eastward, hoping to reach his native Wei, but the border guards stopped him at the gate. Under his own laws, no traveler could pass without proper identification papers, and Shang Yang — the fugitive — dared not reveal his identity. The architect of the surveillance state was caught by his own surveillance. Soldiers dragged him back to Xianyang where King Huiwen ordered the most terrible punishment known to Qin law: death by being tied to five chariots and torn apart. His entire clan was executed alongside him, his works denounced, his name a curse.

But here is the strangest turn in all of Qin history: King Huiwen killed Shang Yang, but he kept every single one of his laws. The Legalist machinery that had transformed Qin was too effective to dismantle. The twenty-grade nobility system remained. The bao-jia surveillance network stayed. The agricultural quotas, the military merit system, the standardized weights and measures — all of it survived the death of its creator. King Huiwen had killed the man but preserved the machine, because the machine worked. For the first time in Chinese history, a state had demonstrated that institutions could outlive individuals — that a system was more powerful than any king, any minister, any dynasty.

第六集：商鞅之死与秦王的崛起

秦孝公于公元前338年夏季去世，狼群果然围拢过来。那位曾被商鞅下令对其师傅施以黥刑的太子嬴驷，此刻登上了王位，是为秦惠文王。他是第一个称"王"而非"公"的秦国国君——这宣告了秦国已与周朝天下的任何强国并驾齐驱。以脸上仍带着刑罚疤痕的公子虔为首的旧贵族，要求复仇。商鞅被控告图谋叛乱而出逃。

他向东方逃亡，希望能回到故国魏国，但边境的士兵将他挡在关前。根据他自己制定的法律，旅客没有身份凭证不能通行，而商鞅此时已是逃犯，不敢暴露身份。监控国家的设计者最终被自己的监控系统抓获。士兵将他拖回咸阳，惠文王下令执行秦律中最可怕的刑罚——车裂，五马分尸。商鞅全族一并被处斩，他的著述被谴责，他的名字成为禁忌。

然而，这是秦国历史上最奇特的转折：秦惠文王杀死了商鞅，却保留了他所制定的每一项法律。那台令秦国脱胎换骨的法家机器太有效了，无法也舍不得拆除。二十等爵制度照旧。保甲连坐网络照旧。农业配额、军功爵位、统一度量衡——发明这一切的人死了，但这套机器全部保留了下来。秦惠文王杀掉了创造者却留下了创造物，因为这台机器确实在运作。这是中国历史上第一次，一个国家证明了制度可以比个人活得更久——一套系统比任何一个国王、任何一个大臣、任何一个朝代都更加强大。

Episode 7: The Horizontal Alliance — Zhang Yi and Qin's Diplomacy

With the Legalist machine running at full power, King Huiwen turned his attention outward. If Shang Yang had built the engine, Qin now needed a driver who could steer it through the treacherous diplomatic landscape of the Warring States. That driver arrived in the person of Zhang Yi, a wandering diplomat who would become one of the most brilliant — and devious — strategists in Chinese history.

Zhang Yi had a simple but devastating formula: horizontal alliance. While his rival Su Qin preached the Vertical Alliance — the six eastern states uniting against Qin — Zhang Yi argued the opposite. Qin should ally with individual eastern states to attack their neighbors, breaking the anti-Qin coalition piece by piece. "Strike the near, befriend the far" became his operational doctrine. He would arrive at a Wei court with an offer of friendship and leave having convinced Wei to attack Chu. He would visit Chu promising shared glory and depart having set Chu against Qi. One by one, he turned the eastern kingdoms against each other, buying time for Qin's military machine to grow stronger.

But diplomacy was only half the equation. In 316 BC, a delegation from the state of Ba — a rich but weak kingdom in the Sichuan basin — begged Qin for military assistance against their aggressive neighbor Shu. The Qin court debated furiously. Some argued that the Sichuan basin was a distant swamp, impossible to govern. Zhang Yi disagreed: "Take Ba and Shu. Their granaries will feed our armies. Their forests will build our fleets. Their rivers flow east into Chu, giving us a back door into the south." King Huiwen approved the expedition. Within the year, both Ba and Shu had fallen to Qin. The Sichuan basin — a territory larger than many of the eastern states combined — was now a Qin province.

第七集：连横——张仪与秦国的外交

法家机器在全速运转，秦惠文王将目光转向对外扩张。如果说商鞅建造了引擎，那么秦国此刻需要的是一位能在战国凶险的外交迷宫中驾驭这引擎的车手。张仪，一位游走列国的纵横家，历史上前无古人后无来者、最出色也最狡诈的战略家之一，应运而来。

张仪的公式简单而致命：连横。他的对手苏秦鼓吹合纵——东方六国团结一致对抗秦国，而张仪的主张恰相反。秦国应与单个东方国家结盟以攻其邻国，将反秦联盟逐片拆解。"远交近攻"成为他的行动纲领。他会带着友谊的提议来到魏国宫廷，离开时已说服魏国攻打楚国。他会拜访楚国，承诺分享荣耀，离去时已让楚国与齐国结仇。一个接一个，他将东方列国彼此推到对立面上，为秦军的战争机器争取了不断壮大自己的时间。

但外交只是方程式的一半。公元前316年，来自巴国——四川盆地中的一个富庶但弱势的王国——的使团哀求秦国出兵援助对抗其强邻蜀国。秦国朝堂展开了激烈辩论。有人认为四川盆地是遥远的泥潭水乡，无力治理。张仪反驳："取巴蜀。那里的粮仓将养育我们的军队，森林将建造我们的舰队，其江水东流入楚，将赐予我们通往南方的后门。"秦惠文王批准了这次远征。不到一年，巴蜀双双臣服于秦。四川盆地——一片比许多东方国家加起来还大的领土——从此成为秦的一个郡。

Episode 8: The Long Reign — King Zhaoxiang's Fifty-Six Years

In 306 BC, a young prince named Ying Ji ascended the Qin throne as King Zhaoxiang. He would reign for fifty-six years — the longest reign of any Warring States monarch — and by the time he died, the balance of power in China would have shifted irreversibly. But he did not begin his reign as the master of his own state. For the first two decades, real power lay with his mother, Queen Dowager Xuan, and her half-brother Wei Ran — brilliant but self-serving politicians who built their own fortunes while keeping the state functional.

It was only in 271 BC that King Zhaoxiang, now middle-aged and weary of being a puppet, met the man who would change everything. Fan Ju was a refugee from Wei, a man who had been beaten nearly to death, stuffed into a latrine, and left to drown in filth by Wei officials jealous of his abilities. He had been smuggled out of Wei in a coffin and brought to Qin by a sympathetic ambassador. Now, standing before King Zhaoxiang, this half-dead scholar delivered the most devastating strategic analysis the Qin court had ever heard.

"For forty years," Fan Ju declared, "Qin has not advanced a single step eastward. Do you know why? It is not because your army is weak. It is because Wei Ran and the Queen Dowager's faction profit from stalemate. Every year of indecisive war enriches them and weakens you. The problem is not the enemy outside — it is the parasites within." This was treasonous speech, and Fan Ju knew it. But King Zhaoxiang, finally seeing his path to true power, seized the moment. In a swift and ruthless purge, he banished Wei Ran, confined the Queen Dowager, and executed her faction's leaders. At the age of fifty-four, Ying Ji became a king in fact as well as in name, and his new prime minister was the man who had been pulled from a latrine.

第八集：漫长的统治——秦昭襄王五十六年

公元前306年，一位名叫嬴稷的年轻王子登上秦国王位，成为秦昭襄王。他的统治将持续五十六年——战国所有君主中最长的一朝——在他去世之时，中国的力量格局已经不可逆转地倾斜了。但他并不是以自己国家主人的身份开始执政的。在最初二十年间，实权掌握在他的母亲宣太后和她的异母弟弟魏冉手中——他们是精明绝顶却自私自利的政客，一边保住国家运转，一边为自己发家致富。

直到公元前271年，秦昭襄王此时已人至中年且厌倦了傀儡生涯，才遇到了那个将改变一切的人。范雎，魏国的流亡者，曾被魏国嫉贤妒能的大臣们几乎打死，塞进茅坑任由粪溺淹没，又被一位心善的秦国使者藏在棺材里走私进了秦境。如今，这位半死的学者站在秦昭襄王面前，说出了一段秦廷闻所未闻的最具摧毁力的战略分析。

范雎断言："四十年来秦在东方不能前进寸步，您可知原因何在？不是秦军不强，而是魏冉和太后一党在僵局中获利。每一场不分胜负的战争，都让他们更富、让您更弱。问题不在于外面的敌人——而在于内部的寄生虫。"这是谋反的言辞，范雎自己心里清楚。但秦昭襄王终于看到了通往实权的道路，牢牢抓住了这个时机。一场迅猛而无情的清洗随之展开——魏冉被放逐，宣太后被软禁，其派系头目全部处死。五十四岁这一年，嬴稷终于成为了名副其实的君主，而他的新任宰相，正是那位从茅坑里被捞出来的人。

Episode 9: The Human Killing Machine — Bai Qi and the Qin Army

Fan Ju brought strategy. But the Qin war machine needed a weapon, and it found one in Bai Qi — the most lethal military commander of the ancient world. Over a thirty-year career, Bai Qi fought more than seventy major battles and never lost a single one. His enemies estimated that his armies killed over one million soldiers and civilians combined. Whether this number was precisified by terrified chroniclers did not matter — what mattered was that everyone in the Warring States world believed it.

Bai Qi's genius was not elegance. He did not write military treatises like Sun Wu, nor did he favor clever ambushes and stratagems. His method was brutally simple: overwhelm. He believed that battles were won before they were fought — in logistics, in training, in the meticulous preparation that the Legalist state made possible. When his army marched, it was preceded by an avalanche of grain, weapons, and replacement troops organized by Fan Ju's administrative machine. When his soldiers attacked, they did so knowing that every head they took meant a promotion, and that retreat meant the execution not just of themselves but of their families.

His greatest triumph — and greatest atrocity — came in 260 BC at Changping. Zhao, the last northern state capable of challenging Qin militarily, had sent an army of nearly half a million men under the cautious but competent commander Lian Po. For three years, the two armies faced each other across a long defensive line in the mountains of Shanxi, neither side willing to commit to a decisive engagement. Then Fan Ju's spies spread a rumor through the Zhao capital of Handan: Lian Po was too timid and would soon surrender. The Zhao king, swallowing the bait, replaced Lian Po with Zhao Kuo, a young general who knew everything about war from books and nothing from experience.

第九集：人屠——白起与秦军

范雎带来了战略。但秦国的战争机器还需要一件武器，这武器就是白起——古代世界最致命的军事指挥官。在三十年的军事生涯中，白起打了七十余场大战，未尝一次败绩。他的敌人统计过，他的军队斩杀士兵与平民总计超过百万人。这个数字是否被惊恐的编年史家夸大了并不重要——重要的是，战国世界的每一个人都相信这个数字。

白起的天才与优雅无关。他不像孙武那样写兵书，也不偏爱巧妙的伏击和诡计。他的方法残酷而简单：压倒一切。他相信战役在开打前就已经赢了——在后勤、在训练、在法家国家机器使之成为可能的缜密准备中。他的军队开拔时，前面是范雎行政机器组织而成的粮草、兵器、替补兵员所形成的洪流。他的士兵冲锋时，心里清楚每一颗首级都意味着晋升，而撤退不仅意味着自己的死刑，也意味着家人的连坐。

他最伟大的胜利——也是最惨烈的暴行——发生在公元前260年的长平。赵国，北方最后一个能在军事上匹敌秦国的国家，派出了近五十万大军，由谨慎但称职的将军廉颇率领。三年间，两军在山西山地沿漫长的防线对峙，谁都不愿投入一场决胜负的战斗。就在这时，范雎的间谍在赵国首都邯郸散播了流言：廉颇太懦弱，快要投降了。赵王咬下了这个诱饵，用赵括替换了廉颇。这位年轻将军从书本上学到了一切兵法，却对实战一无所知。

Episode 10: Changping — The Battle That Decided China

Zhao Kuo arrived at the Changping front with grand proclamations and orders to attack immediately. Bai Qi, feigning a retreat, baited the young general into charging his forces deep into a valley where Qin troops had secretly circled behind them. The trap snapped shut with terrifying speed. Bai Qi's cavalry sealed the valley entrance; his infantry built walls across the mountain passes. Zhao Kuo's entire force was surrounded, cut off from supply, starving within days.

For forty-six days, the Zhao army held out in that mountain valley. They ate their horses, then their leather armor, then — the chronicles whisper — they ate their dead. Zhao Kuo himself was killed attempting a breakout, cut down by Qin arrows. On the forty-seventh day, the remaining Zhao troops — some two hundred and forty thousand men — surrendered. Bai Qi stood on a ridge overlooking the sea of kneeling soldiers and made a decision that would echo through Chinese history like a scream. "The Zhao soldiers are not reliable," he informed the Qin court. "They will rebel at the first opportunity. The only way to prevent future trouble is to kill them all."

He ordered every Zhao prisoner executed and buried. According to Sima Qian, writing a century later, four hundred thousand men were slaughtered and thrown into mass graves. Modern archaeology at the Changping site has confirmed the existence of enormous burial pits filled with skeletons bearing the marks of violent death. The news of Changping struck the Warring States world like an earthquake. When the Zhao mothers heard it, it was said that the wailing from Handan could be heard in the Qin capital of Xianyang. But the strategic calculus was cold: Zhao had lost its entire military-age male population. No state now remained that could stand alone against Qin. The die was cast for unification.

第十集：长平——决定中国命运的战役

赵括来到了长平前线，带来了宏大的宣言和立刻进攻的命令。白起佯装溃退，引诱年轻将军将部队深入一个山谷，秦军早已悄悄绕到了他们的后方。陷阱以可怕的速度合拢。白起的骑兵封住了谷口，步兵封锁了山间关隘。赵括全军被包围，补给断绝，数日内便陷入绝粮之境。

整整四十六天，赵军在峡谷中坚守。他们先是吃马，然后吃皮甲，再然后——史书低声记载——吃了死去的战友。赵括本人尝试突围时被秦弩射杀。第四十七天，残余的赵军将士——约二十四万人——投降。白起站在山脊上俯瞰跪倒一片的降兵，做出了一个在中国历史上如尖叫般回荡不去的决定。他向秦廷禀告："赵卒不可信，稍有机会就会反叛。除日后之患，唯有尽杀之。"

他下令将每一名赵国俘虏全部处决掩埋。据一个世纪后司马迁记载，四十万人被屠戮并投入万人坑。长平遗址的现代考古已证实存在大量葬坑，坑中骸骨遍布暴力致死痕迹。长平的消息如地震般传遍战国世界。赵国母亲听到此事后，据说从邯郸传来的悲恸嚎哭声在秦都咸阳都听得到。但战略计算冷酷而精准：赵国失去了全部适龄男性兵员，没有任何一个国家能单独与秦军抗衡了。统一的骰子已经掷下。

Episode 11: The Last Kings — From Zhaoxiang to the Boy Monarch

King Zhaoxiang died in 251 BC at the age of seventy-five, having outlived his eldest son and seen four generations of Qin princes come and go. His reign had transformed Qin from a powerful state into an empire-in-waiting. The army had been perfected by Bai Qi. The administrative machinery had been streamlined by Fan Ju. The granaries overflowed. The eastern states were broken and terrified. And yet Zhaoxiang did not attempt unification. He had torn the legs from every rival but stopped short of delivering the killing blow. Perhaps he was too old. Perhaps he understood that conquest and governance were different arts, and that his generation's task was to build the weapon, not fire it.

Zhaoxiang was succeeded by his son, known posthumously as King Xiaowen, who died within three days of his coronation — possibly poisoned, possibly ill, the records are unclear. His son, King Zhuangxiang, reigned for three lackluster years, notable only for placing the state's finances in the hands of a brilliant merchant named Lü Buwei. When Zhuangxiang died in 247 BC, the throne passed to his thirteen-year-old son, a boy born in a foreign land, raised under a false name, surrounded by intrigue. His birth name was Ying Zheng. Later, the world would call him Qin Shi Huang — the First Emperor.

But in those early years, Zheng was a boy king in a palace of predators. Lü Buwei served as regent, styling himself the king's "second father" — a claim that court whispers suggested might be literally true. And in the shadows lurked another figure: Lao Ai, a man who had been planted in the palace as the Queen Dowager's lover, building his own private army, his own network of loyalists, waiting for the moment to strike. The court of Qin had become a nest of vipers, and the boy at the center of it all was learning the most dangerous lesson a monarch can learn: trust no one.

第十一集：最后的君王——从昭襄王到少年君主

秦昭襄王于公元前251年去世，享年七十五岁。他的长子已先他而去，他亲眼目睹了四代秦国公子来来去去。他的统治将一个强大的秦国变成了一个翘首以盼的新帝国。军队被白起淬炼至臻完美，行政机器被范雎打磨得精干高效，粮仓满溢，东方各国残败而惊恐。然而，秦昭襄王并没有尝试统一天下。他已扯断了每一位对手的双腿，却没有发出最后的致命一击。也许是他太老了，也许他深知征服与治理是截然不同的两种艺术，而他们这一代人的任务是铸造武器，而不是扣动扳机。

昭襄王由他的儿子继位，谥号秦孝文王。他在加冕三天内便去世了——可能是被毒杀，也可能是疾病的缘故，史书并无明确记载。他的儿子秦庄襄王又执政三年，政绩平平，唯一值得注意的举动是将国家财政交给了一位叫吕不韦的精明商人打理。庄襄王于公元前247年去世，王位传给了他十三岁的儿子，一个生于异国他乡、以假名养育、被阴谋包围的男孩。他的本名是嬴政。后来，这个世界将称他为秦始皇。

但在登基的头几年里，嬴政只是一个被放在掠食者环绕的王座上、孤立无援的少年。吕不韦以相国摄政的身份自称为王的"仲父"——这个称谓在宫中窃窃私语中被暗示可能是实打实的真话。而在暗处还潜伏着另一个人物：嫪毐，一个被安插进宫中、成为太后情人的男人。他正在打造自己的私人军队、自己的忠诚党派，等待出手的时刻。秦国的宫廷已变成了一个毒蛇窝，处于漩涡中心的那个少年，正在学习一个君主所能学到的最危险的一课：永远不要相信任何人。

Episode 12: The Boy King Takes Power — Ying Zheng's Purge

In 238 BC, Ying Zheng came of age and performed the capping ceremony at the old Qin capital of Yong. Under Legalist law, the king was now sovereign in his own right — no more regents, no more "second fathers." Lao Ai, sensing that his time was running out, made his move. Using the Queen Dowager's seal, he forged royal orders mobilizing troops and led a revolt against the young king, hoping to seize the throne before Zheng could consolidate power.

But the boy king had not been idle. Zheng had been watching, waiting, learning the patterns of his enemies. He had quietly built his own network of loyal generals and ministers — men like Li Si, a Legalist scholar who had studied under the great Xunzi, and whose mind was as sharp as Shang Yang's. When Lao Ai's rebels marched on the Qinian Palace, they found the king's forces waiting for them. The battle in Xianyang's streets was brief and savage. Lao Ai's army was shattered, and he was captured, executed by being tied to five chariots and torn apart — the same punishment that had killed Shang Yang. His entire clan was exterminated. The Queen Dowager was placed under house arrest for the remainder of her life.

But Zheng was not finished. The investigation into Lao Ai's conspiracy led inexorably to Lü Buwei, the regent who had ruled Qin for a decade. Whether Lü Buwei had known of the plot was never proven. It did not matter. Zheng exiled him to Shu in the distant southwest, and a year later, Lü Buwei drank poison — whether by his own hand or another's, the records do not say. The young king had cleaned house with a ruthlessness that would have impressed even King Zhaoxiang. At the age of twenty-two, Ying Zheng was now the undisputed master of the most powerful state the Chinese world had ever known. And he had a plan.

第十二集：少年君主的掌权——嬴政的清洗

公元前238年，嬴政成年，在秦旧都雍城举行了加冠礼。按照法家法律，国王现在是自己王国的真正主人了——从此不再有摄政者，不再有所谓的"仲父"。嫪毐感到自己的时间不多了，发动了政变。他用太后的印章伪造了王令，调集军队发动了针对年轻国王的叛乱，试图在嬴政巩固权力之前夺取王位。

但这个少年国王并非无所作为。嬴政一直在观察、在等待、在钻研敌人的规律。他已悄悄建立起了自己的忠诚将领和谋臣网络——像李斯这样的人，一位师从大儒荀子的法家学者，其智谋锋利如商鞅一般。当嫪毐的叛军向蕲年宫进军时，他们发现迎接他们的是国王早已准备好的精兵。咸阳街头的战斗短暂而残酷。嫪毐的军队被粉碎，他本人被捕，以五马分尸处死——与当年杀死商鞅同样的刑罚。他的全族被夷灭。太后被软禁，了此残生。

但嬴政还没有结束。对嫪毐阴谋的调查不可避免地牵连到了吕不韦，那位统治了秦国十年之久的摄政丞相。吕不韦是否知情从未被证实。但这已经不重要了。嬴政将他流放到遥远的西南蜀地。一年后，吕不韦饮下毒药——究竟是自己的手还是别人的手，史书未言明。年轻的国王以连秦昭襄王都要为之侧目的冷酷完成了一次大清洗。二十二岁的嬴政如今毫无争议地成为了中国世界有史以来最强国家的唯一主宰。而他心中，早已有了一个计划。

Episode 13: The Plan for Unification — Li Si, Wang Jian, and the Strategy

With the court purged and power consolidated, Ying Zheng gathered his inner circle in the palace of Xianyang. The question was no longer whether Qin would conquer the six remaining states, but how and in what order. Li Si, now the king's chief strategist, laid out a plan of breathtaking simplicity: start with the weakest, isolate the strongest, and never give them time to reunite.

The first target was Han, the smallest and most strategically vulnerable of the six. Wedged between Qin in the west and the other states in the east, Han had survived for centuries only through diplomacy — playing its powerful neighbors against each other. But now the game was over. There was no ally left to save it. The second target would be Zhao, still reeling from Changping and now ruled by a weak king beset by internal intrigue. Then Wei, the state that had once challenged Qin for supremacy but had been bled white by decades of border wars. Then Chu, the giant of the south, vast in territory but weak in central authority. Then Yan, isolated in the remote northeast. And finally Qi, the rich trading power in the east that had watched its allies fall one by one while maintaining a policy of neutral appeasement.

To execute this plan, Zheng turned to the greatest general of his generation: Wang Jian. Unlike Bai Qi, who crushed enemies with overwhelming force, Wang Jian was a master of psychological warfare and patience. He would spend months, sometimes years, maneuvering an enemy into a hopeless position before striking. He asked openly for rewards — land, gold, titles — not because he was greedy, but because he knew the king trusted a general who wanted something. An ambitious general was predictable; a general who wanted nothing was terrifying. And so began the eleven-year conquest that would reshape the world.

第十三集：统一计划——李斯、王翦与大战略

朝廷清洗完毕、权力集中稳固之后，嬴政聚集了核心圈重臣于咸阳宫中。问题不再是秦国要不要征服剩余的六国，而是怎样征服、以什么顺序。李斯，如今是国王的首席谋士，提出了一份令人叹为观止、简单直白的计划：先打最弱的，孤立最强的，永远不给它们重新联合的机会。

第一目标是韩国，六国中最小、战略上最脆弱的。韩国夹在西面的秦与东面诸国之间，数百年来仅凭外交存活，指使列强互相牵制。但现在游戏结束了，没有任何盟友能救它了。第二目标是赵国，长平之战后尚未恢复元气，如今又由一个软弱的君主统治，内部倾轧不断。然后是魏国，曾与秦争锋的国家，但几十年的边境战争已让它流干了血。然后是楚国，南方的巨无霸，领土辽阔但中央权威虚弱。然后是燕国，偏处东北、孤立无援。最后是齐国，东方以贸易致富的大国，在各盟国一个接一个倒下时始终奉行中立绥靖政策。

为了执行这个计划，嬴政启用了这一代最伟大的战将——王翦。与白起以压倒性力量粉碎敌人不同，王翦是心理战和耐心的艺术大师。他会花上几个月、甚至几年，将敌人一步步逼到绝境后才会出手。他公然讨赏——要土地、要黄金、要爵位，不是因为他贪婪，而是因为他深知国王信任有所求的将军。有野心的将军是可预测的，而一无所求的将军才真正令人恐惧。由此开始了这场历时十一年、将重新塑造整个世界的征服之战。

Episode 14: The Eleven-Year Conquest — Six Kingdoms Fall

The conquest began in 230 BC with Han. It was over almost before it began. Qin forces marched east through the Hangu Pass and swept into the Han capital of Xinzheng. The Han king was captured and his territory absorbed as the Yingchuan commandery. One kingdom down, five to go. The Zhao campaign of 229-228 BC was harder. The Zhao general Li Mu had been the only commander in the east to win consistently against Qin forces. Wang Jian knew better than to fight Li Mu on equal terms. Instead, Qin spies bribed Zhao officials to spread a rumor that Li Mu was planning to defect. The Zhao king arrested and executed his last great general. With Li Mu dead, Wang Jian walked into the Zhao capital of Handan virtually unopposed. Zheng visited the city of his childhood — the place where he had spent his boyhood as a hostage — and ordered the execution of every person who had wronged his family. The Wei campaign of 225 BC was an engineering spectacle. Wang Ben diverted the Yellow River to flood the Wei capital of Daliang, reducing its walls to mud in weeks. King Wei surrendered. The Chu campaign was the war's great crisis. Wang Jian demanded an army of six hundred thousand men — the largest force ever assembled in Chinese history — and the young general Li Xin, eager for glory, mocked him for cowardice. Zheng gave Li Xin two hundred thousand to attack Chu, and the young general was annihilated. Zheng personally drove to Wang Jian's estate and begged him to return. With six hundred thousand men, Wang Jian invaded Chu, spent a year winning the loyalty of its people through exemplary discipline, and then crushed the Chu army in a single decisive battle. Yan fell in 222 BC after a brief and desperate resistance. Finally, in 221 BC, Qi surrendered without a battle. After five centuries of continuous war, China was unified under a single ruler.

第十四集：十一年征服——六国覆灭

公元前230年灭韩。征服几乎刚开始就结束了。秦军东出函谷关进兵韩都新郑，韩王被俘，其领土被划入颍川郡。一亡，余五。公元前229至228年伐赵，战事远为艰辛。赵将李牧是东方唯一能屡屡战胜秦军的统帅。王翦深知不能与李牧公平对垒，于是派出秦国间谍贿赂赵国官员，散布流言说李牧密谋叛变。赵王逮捕并处决了自己最后一位伟大的将军。李牧一死，王翦几乎未遇有效抵抗便走进了赵国都城邯郸。嬴政回到了他长大的城市——那个他作为人质度过童年的地方——下令处死了每一个曾经伤害过他家人的仇敌。公元前225年灭魏，这是一场工程奇观。王贲掘开黄河淹没了魏国都城大梁，城墙数周之内化成了泥浆。魏王投降。攻楚是整场战争中最大的危机。王翦要求统兵六十万——中国历史上前所未有、最大规模的军队，年轻的战将李信渴望建功，嘲笑王翦胆怯。嬴政给了李信二十万人伐楚，这位年轻将军全军覆没。嬴政亲自驱车至王翦宅前恳求他复出。王翦率六十万大军伐楚，用了一年时间以模范军纪赢得楚国民心，而后一战定乾坤。燕国于公元前222年在短暂而绝望的抵抗后灭亡。最后，公元前221年，齐国不战而降。经过五个世纪绵延不断的战争，中国第一次统一在了一个君主之下。

Episode 15: The First Emperor — Remaking a World

In 221 BC, Ying Zheng stood at the apex of the known world. He had conquered six kingdoms in eleven years, a pace of conquest unmatched in history. But he understood that military victory was only the beginning. The problem that now confronted him was unprecedented: how to govern the largest territorial state the Chinese world had ever seen, comprising peoples who spoke different dialects, wrote in different scripts, used different currencies, and hated each other with the passion of centuries.

Zheng gathered his ministers and posed the question that would define his legacy: what title should a man who has accomplished more than all the sage kings of antiquity call himself? After debate, the decision was made: Shi Huangdi, the First Emperor. He would not be a mere king — the Zhou had kings by the dozen. He was something new, something that had never existed before. The title itself was a declaration that history had entered a new epoch.

Then came the standardization. A single system of weights and measures replaced the bewildering local variations that had made trade a nightmare for centuries. A single currency — the round coin with a square hole — replaced the knives, spades, and cowrie shells of the former kingdoms. A single standardized script, the Small Seal Script devised by Li Si, replaced the regional variants that had made written communication between states a matter of guesswork. The axles of carts were ordered to be a uniform width so that the roads of the empire would wear evenly. And the Great Wall — connecting, extending, and reinforcing the northern fortifications of the former kingdoms — rose as a statement of imperial will, built by the forced labor of hundreds of thousands of convicts and conscripts.

第十五集：始皇帝——重塑一个世界

公元前221年，嬴政站在了已知世界的顶点。他用十一年征服了六个王国，这种征服速度前无古人后无来者。但他深知，军事胜利只是开端。摆在他面前的难题是史无前例的：如何治理中国历史上领土最广袤的国家，这个国家由一群说着不同方言、写着不同文字、使用不同货币、且彼此间积累了数百年仇恨的人民组成。

嬴政召集众臣，提出了一个将定义他历史地位的问题：一个成就超过了古代所有圣王的君主，应被冠以何种称号？经过一番争论，决定了——始皇帝。他将不仅仅是一个"王"——周朝有成打的王。他是全新的、前所未有的存在。这个头衔本身就是一个宣言：历史已进入了一个新时代。

随之而来的是大一统的标准化体系。统一的度量衡取代了各诸侯国纷繁的地方量制，这些量制让商业在数百年间蒙受无尽混乱。统一的货币——外圆内方的方孔圆钱——取代了各诸侯王国曾流通的刀币、铲币和贝币。统一的文字小篆，由李斯设计制定，取代了各地域的文字变体，使得跨邦域的文字通信从此不再是猜谜。所有车辆的轮距被强制统一，以确保帝国道路均匀磨损。而万里长城则在各旧诸侯国北部防线的基础上被连接、延伸、加固，拔地而起。这是帝国意志的宏大宣言，动用了数十万囚徒和征夫的血肉浇筑而成。

Episode 16: The Legacy — What Qin Built, What Qin Destroyed

The First Emperor's reign lasted only eleven years after unification. In that brief span, he constructed an imperial system whose basic architecture would endure for two thousand years. The empire was divided into thirty-six commanderies, each governed by officials appointed and removable by the central court — the administrative model that every subsequent Chinese dynasty would adopt. He built a network of imperial highways radiating from Xianyang that allowed armies and messages to move across the empire with unprecedented speed. He standardized everything he could touch: the width of roads, the language of law, the measurement of land.

But the cost was immense and the anger it generated was even greater. The Legalist machinery that had conquered China proved far better at crushing resistance than at winning loyalty. Hundreds of thousands of laborers died building the Great Wall and the imperial roads. Scholars who dared to criticize the regime's policies found their books burned and themselves buried alive. And the First Emperor, increasingly obsessed with death and immortality, poured unimaginable wealth into the construction of his tomb — an underground city guarded by an army of terracotta warriors, whose discovery two thousand years later would astonish the world.

In 210 BC, on an inspection tour of the eastern provinces, the First Emperor fell ill and died. The circumstances of his death were immediately shrouded in conspiracy. Li Si and the eunuch Zhao Gao concealed the death for weeks — the body decomposing in a cart loaded with salted fish to mask the smell — while they forged an edict ordering the crown prince Fusu to commit suicide and installing the pliable younger son Huhai on the throne. Within three years of the emperor's death, the empire collapsed in a storm of peasant rebellions. And yet Qin's legacy refused to die with its dynasty. Every unified Chinese empire that followed — Han, Tang, Song, Ming — would trace its institutional DNA back to the Legalist state that had risen from the western dust. The name "China" itself in Western languages derives from Qin. The First Emperor had failed to build an eternal dynasty, but he succeeded in building an eternal idea: that China was one.

第十六集：遗产——秦所创建、秦所毁灭

始皇帝统一后的统治仅持续了十一年。在如此短暂的跨度中，他构建了一套帝国体系，其基本架构延续了两千年。帝国划分为三十六郡，各郡由中央任命并可随时罢免的官员治理——这套行政模式为后世每一个中国王朝所沿用。他建设了从咸阳辐射而出的帝国驰道网络，使大军与信息以前所未有的速度穿梭于帝国疆域之上。他将一切能碰得到的东西标准化了：道路的宽度、法律的语言、土地的度量。

但代价是巨大的，怒火更加巨大。那台征服了中国的法家机器，在粉碎反抗方面远比在赢得民心方面更加有效。数十万劳工在修建长城和帝国驰道时丧命。敢于批评朝廷政策的学者，其书被焚、其人被埋。而始皇帝对死亡和不朽的执念日益严重，倾注了不可计量的财富营建他的陵寝——一个由兵马俑军队护卫的地下王城。其发现令两千年后的世界为之震撼。

公元前210年，始皇帝在巡视东方行省途中病倒并去世。其死亡处境立即被笼罩在阴谋之中。李斯与宦官赵高将皇帝的死讯隐瞒了数周——尸体在满载咸鱼的车上腐烂以掩盖臭味——同时伪造诏书令太子扶苏自杀，立软弱可欺的少子胡亥继位。始皇帝死后不出三年，帝国便在农民起义的怒潮中崩塌了。然而，秦的遗产拒绝随王朝一起消亡。此后每一个统一的中国王朝——汉、唐、宋、明——都将制度基因追溯至那从西方尘土中崛起的法家之国。而"China"这个词本身即源自"A"Qin。始皇帝未能建立一个永恒的王朝，但他成功建立一个永恒的信念：中国，是一个。
